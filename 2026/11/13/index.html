Best Practical Business Item

Price before you promise.

The SBA resource-partner workshop “Pricing for Profit” is online Monday, November 16, 12:30–2:30 PM Central. The fee is $200. It treats pricing as both math and market positioning—costs, customer value, pricing structures and next steps.

Why it matters: Before quoting the next job, know the direct-cost floor, the price that pays for overhead and owner time, and the price the customer can connect to a clear result.

Vermont / Working Lands

Use the November 17 Q&A to test one project.

Vermont's Business Enhancement Grant remains open through December 10, 2026, at 2:00 PM. Awards are $10,000–$50,000, no match is required, and the next virtual Q&A is Tuesday, November 17, 3:00–4:00 PM. Project-planning help is limited and first-come, first-served.

Why it matters: Bring a one-sentence bottleneck, the proposed fix, a rough budget and one measurable result.

Forest Innovation / Eligibility

Do not spend time on Phase II unless you already won Phase I.

USDA NIFA's SBIR/STTR Phase II opportunity closes November 17. Awards are $600,000–$650,000 with no match, but only prior USDA SBIR/STTR Phase I winners are eligible.

Why it matters: Confirm the prior Phase I award before doing any application work.

Digital Risk

Name the recovery steps before an AI-enabled scam finds the weak spot.

The SBA lists a free online webinar November 17, 10:00–11:00 AM Eastern on AI-enabled cybersecurity risks. It covers practical safeguards, recent incidents and recovery planning; no technical background is required.

Why it matters: Write down who controls administrator accounts, where the clean backup lives, whether multi-factor authentication is on, and who can restore critical operations.

Operations Checkpoint

Monthly payroll-tax deposits are due November 17.

The IRS calendar lists November 17 for Social Security, Medicare and withheld income tax on October payments when the monthly deposit rule applies. EFTPS users must schedule payments by 8:00 PM Eastern at least one calendar day before the due date.

Why it matters: Confirm the deposit schedule that applies and save the payment confirmation with the payroll reports.
