What is useful this week

A December deadline board, Vermont timber-disaster assistance, Rural Small Business award nominations and a cold-weather crew plan.

Best Practical Business Item

Build one deadline board for the first half of December.

Put the owner, next action and hard stop beside every live application. Three near-term dates deserve attention: December 7 at 3:00 PM Eastern for SBA National Small Business Week award nominations, December 10 at 2:00 PM for Vermont Working Lands Business Enhancement Grants, and December 31 for Vermont timber-disaster assistance.

Why it matters: Deadlines become expensive when they live in separate inboxes. One visible board makes the choice explicit: submit, delegate or stop.

Vermont / Timber Recovery

VATDAP timber-loss applications close December 31.

Vermont’s Timber Disaster Assistance Program covers eligible 2023–24 weather losses for forest landowners, forestland lessees and loggers with a financial interest in the timber. The state lists standing and harvested timber losses, qualifying management-plan updates, eligible costs to resume logging and reestablish AMP compliance, plus licensed-forester assessment costs.

Do now: Confirm that the event and loss category qualify, then assemble photos, contracts, invoices, salvage-payment records and the forester assessment before starting the final application.

Recognition / Rural Business

The Rural Small Business of the Year nomination deadline is December 7.

SBA allows self-nominations and nominations by organizations that support small businesses. The rural category requires a qualifying rural address, at least three years in operation, majority ownership by the nominee and prior SBA-supported assistance. The package is due by 3:00 PM Eastern on December 7.

Why it matters: If a business has the record, do not leave the case in people’s heads. Gather the financial-growth evidence, community impact, photo and supporting letters into one complete package.

Vermont / Working Lands

Use the December 2 office hour before the December 10 grant deadline.

Vermont’s Business Enhancement Grant is open to eligible farm, forest-products and local-food businesses for $10,000–$50,000 projects involving equipment, infrastructure, markets, workforce or research and development. The application closes December 10 at 2:00 PM; the state’s schedule lists virtual Q&A sessions on December 2 and December 9.

Do now: Bring one unresolved eligibility or application question to the December 2 session. Do not wait for the final office hour to discover a missing quote, registration or scope problem.

Winter Operations

Write the cold-weather stop-work plan before the next storm.

OSHA recommends training crews to recognize cold stress, using warm break areas and safe work practices, monitoring weather and maintaining reliable communication—especially for remote work. Turn those points into a one-page plan with a supervisor, check-in interval, warm-up trigger and stop-work authority.

Why it matters: “Be careful” is not a control. A named decision rule helps a crew act before judgment, dexterity or communication starts to fail.

Which December deadline deserves a “no”?

Name the opportunity, the owner, the constraint it solves and the evidence that makes it more valuable than the work it will displace.
